1. Summary and verdict
Verdict: almost ready to go live. The site is ready once six must-fix items are closed, about half a day of work.
All 55 pages were reviewed. The copy is strong and consistent in tone, with no typos, placeholder text or broken pages
found. The remaining problems are inconsistent facts (years of experience, ISO wording, address), a few over-strong
claims, and the domain switch-over from vercel.app to kneo.in.

and MES demos.
