Scope

What we do on site.

Installation & coordination

Mechanical-electrical coordination, positioning, services connection and interface management with other trades.

I/O validation

Point-by-point verification of every input and output against the I/O list before any sequence is enabled.

PLC, SCADA & robot debugging

Live debugging by the engineers who wrote the code — not a support desk reading it for the first time.

Safety testing

Functional verification of guarding, interlocks, e-stops and safety-rated circuits, documented for acceptance.

Line balancing

Tuning station timings and buffer behaviour so the line runs to takt rather than to its slowest untuned station.

Production ramp-up

Supported production trials, yield stabilisation and handover once the line is holding rate and quality.
