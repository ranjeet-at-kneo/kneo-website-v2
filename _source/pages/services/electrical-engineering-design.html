The problem

Bad drawings cost more than bad code.

A software fault gets fixed in an afternoon. An unlabelled terminal, a cable schedule that does not match the panel, or a safety circuit that cannot be validated will cost days on site — and will keep costing every time the machine is serviced.

Most electrical documentation problems are not technical. They come from drawings produced by someone who has never had to commission from them.

Our approach: every drawing set is produced by engineers who also commission machines. If a schematic would be painful to work from at 3am on a shutdown, it does not leave our office.

What we deliver

The complete documentation set.

Electrical schematics

ePlan and AutoCAD electrical schematics with consistent cross-referencing and page structure to IEC 81346-1 designations.

Wiring & cable documentation

Wiring diagrams, cable schedules and terminal charts that match the physical build — not an idealised version of it.

Panel layout & BOM

Control panel layouts with thermal and clearance consideration, plus a purchasable bill of materials with alternates identified.

Safety circuit design

Safety architecture designed and documented to the required SIL or Performance Level, with the calculation trail attached.

Power & control circuits

Supply distribution, protection coordination, motor circuits, VFD integration and control voltage design.

PLC I/O planning

I/O allocation, module selection, spare capacity planning and the I/O list that controls software will actually be written against.
