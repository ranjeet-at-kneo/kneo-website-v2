The problem

Islands of data, none of them talking.

Most plants have a controller that knows the machine state, a historian nobody queries, a quality spreadsheet on someone's laptop and an ERP that finds out what happened a day late. Each system is correct. Together they tell you nothing.

Ignition's licensing model and web-based architecture make it a practical way to unify those islands without paying per client, per tag or per screen.
