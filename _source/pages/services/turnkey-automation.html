Overview

Automation has become versatile enough to fit your process.

Advances in robotics and software have made automation more versatile, reliable and easier to upgrade than ever before. That flexibility allows us to build robotic handling systems across a wide range of applications — boosting productivity and opening new opportunities for your business.

The alternative — buying a standard machine and reshaping your process around it — is usually where automation projects quietly lose their business case.

Delivery

How a turnkey project runs.

01

Process study & concept

Cycle-time targets, part variants, layout constraints, safety category and a costed concept before commitment.

02

Detailed design

Mechanical design, gripper and fixture engineering, ePlan schematics, I/O lists and safety architecture.

03

Build & FAT

Cell assembly, panel wiring and functional acceptance testing against the agreed specification.

04

Virtual commissioning

Robot paths, interlocks and sequences validated against a digital twin — collisions resolved in software.

05

Installation & SAT

Site installation, I/O validation, safety testing, dry cycles and production trials.

06

Handover & support

Operator and maintenance training, complete documentation and an agreed ongoing support arrangement.

Benefits

Why turnkey rather than piecemeal.

Single accountability

One contract, one team. Nobody can point at another supplier when the cell does not hit rate.

Faster ramp-up

Mechanical, electrical and software decisions made together instead of negotiated between vendors.

Built to be upgraded

Modern robotics and software make later re-tooling for a new product far less disruptive.

Simple to operate

Complexity is absorbed in engineering, not passed to the operator on the shift.
