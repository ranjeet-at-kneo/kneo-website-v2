Delivery model

How a turnkey project runs.

01

Concept & feasibility

Process study, cycle-time targets, layout constraints, safety category assessment and a costed concept before commitment.

02

Detailed engineering

Mechanical interfaces, ePlan schematics, I/O lists, safety architecture, panel design and BOM release for procurement.

03

Build & panel FAT

Panels wired, labelled and functionally tested against the I/O list; robot and machine assembly progressed in parallel.

04

Software & virtual commissioning

PLC, HMI, SCADA and robot programs written offline and validated against a digital twin — collisions and sequence errors resolved before shipment.

05

Site installation & SAT

Mechanical-electrical coordination, I/O validation, safety testing, dry cycles, then production trials and line balancing.

06

Ramp-up, training & handover

Operator and maintenance training, documentation pack, ramp-up support and an agreed support arrangement going forward.
