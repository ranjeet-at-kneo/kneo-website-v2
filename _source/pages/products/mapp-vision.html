The problem

Human inspection is inconsistent by design, not by fault.

Visual inspection depends on attention, lighting, fatigue and how many parts the operator has already looked at that shift. Escape rates rise late in a shift for reasons that have nothing to do with competence.

Rule-based vision handles well-defined features. Defects that vary in appearance — scratches, surface texture, subtle misalignment — are where AI-based classification earns its place.

Key features

What MAPP Vision does.

Defect detection & classification

Identifies scratches, dents, misalignments, missing parts, wrong orientation, labelling issues and surface defects — and classifies them so the response can be automated rather than escalated.

AI model training & controlled improvement

Rule-based or AI-based inspection with manual retraining whenever new defect images or scenarios are added — so performance changes deliberately, not silently.

High-speed real-time processing

Designed for cycle-time-critical applications, making an accept/reject decision inside the station's available window.

On-premise deployment

Inspections execute on local infrastructure. Images and models stay inside your plant network.

Predictable by design: models are retrained deliberately when new defect scenarios are added, rather than adapting continuously — so behaviour on a validated line does not change without someone approving it.
