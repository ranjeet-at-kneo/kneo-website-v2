The challenge

Improvement spread evenly across a mill achieves almost nothing.

Textile operations run large numbers of similar machines, which makes averages deceptive. A plant-level efficiency figure can look acceptable while a minority of assets quietly consume most of the lost output.

Machine-level measurement changes the arithmetic entirely - and usually reveals that the constraint is concentrated in far fewer assets than expected.

Our starting point is always the same: instrument the process, agree what good looks like, then automate. Visibility first, optimisation second.
