The challenge

Automated storage is not a shelving upgrade.

An ASRS changes how inventory, labour and order profiles interact. It also exposes every assumption buried in the warehouse management system - including the ones that were tolerable when a person could improvise around them.

Successful projects treat the physical automation and the information flow as one design problem, not two separate procurements.

Our starting point is always the same: instrument the process, agree what good looks like, then automate. Visibility first, optimisation second.
