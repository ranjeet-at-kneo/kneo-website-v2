The challenge

Cycle time and traceability decide whether the plant hits its build rate.

Automotive plants operate to a takt that leaves no slack. A station running two seconds over specification does not cause a visible failure - it quietly caps the line, shift after shift, until someone measures it.

At the same time, customer agreements increasingly require per-unit genealogy on demand. Both problems have the same root: production data that exists inside the equipment but never reaches anyone who can act on it.

Our starting point is always the same: instrument the process, agree what good looks like, then automate. Visibility first, optimisation second.
