Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information collected through this website and during commercial engagements.

Summary: we collect only what you send us through the contact form or by email, we use it solely to respond to your enquiry and manage our commercial relationship, and we do not sell it.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by KNEO Automation Pvt. Ltd. ("KNEO", "we", "us"), with offices at Office no. 502–504, 5th Floor, Highway Heights, Warje, Pune – 411058, Maharashtra, India, and Plot No 7, Bhuvaneswari Nagar, First Street, Alapakkam Road, Valasarvakkam, Chennai – 600087, Tamil Nadu, India.

For any question about this policy or about the information we hold, contact sales@kneoautomation.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide. Name, company, email address, telephone number, requirement category and the content of any message you send through our contact form or by email.
  • Recruitment information. Where you apply for a role, the CV and supporting information you send to our recruitment address.
  • Technical information. Standard server log data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, generated automatically when any website is accessed.
  • Preference information. Your chosen colour theme is stored locally in your browser. It never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the information, quotation or consultation you asked for.
  • To manage an ongoing commercial relationship, including project delivery and support.
  • To assess applications for employment where you have applied to us.
  • To maintain the security and correct operation of this website.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations that apply to us.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use enquiry data for unrelated marketing without your agreement.

4. Legal basis

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (given when you submit a form and confirm this policy), on the performance of a contract or steps taken at your request before entering one, and on our legitimate interest in operating and securing our business.

5. Sharing

We share personal information only where necessary: with service providers who host or support our systems and act on our instructions; with professional advisers; and where required by law or by a competent authority. Any onward transfer is limited to what the purpose requires.

6. Retention

Enquiry correspondence is retained for as long as needed to respond and, where a commercial relationship follows, for the duration of that relationship plus any period required for legal, contractual or accounting purposes. Recruitment information is retained only for as long as it is relevant to current or foreseeable openings.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed completely secure, and you send information to us at your own risk.

Where KNEO processes customer production data as part of a project — for example within an on-premise MAPP deployment — the handling, residency and security of that data are governed by the project contract rather than by this website policy.

8. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a single preference value in your browser's local storage to remember whether you selected the light or dark theme. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings without affecting how the site works.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, ask us to delete it where we have no continuing need to retain it, or withdraw consent you previously gave. To make a request, email sales@kneoautomation.com.

10. External links

This website links to external sites, including our blog and our LinkedIn page. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of sites we do not operate. Please review their policies separately.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page is the one that applies.
