Electrical Engineering & Design

Robot, PLC, HMI & SCADA Programming

Siemens · Rockwell · Beckhoff · Mitsubishi · Delta

Whatever brand, whatever complexity — discrete, batch and process control, smart data collection, vision/robot/motion integration, PLC-to-PC communication and recipe management.

Turnkey Solutions for Automation Systems

Design → Build → Handover

Complete automation cells engineered to handle complex requirements while remaining simple to operate — materials handling, machine tending, palletizing, assembly, vision inspection and more.

Virtual Commissioning & Digital Twin

Emulation · Simulation · Risk-free testing

Catch glitches, collisions and programming errors before your system touches the shop floor. Faster startup, fewer surprises during installation, and day-one performance that holds to plan.

 05

Ignition SCADA Solutions

Perspective · Vision · OPC UA · MES / OEE

End-to-end Ignition SCADA that connects machines, PLCs, sensors and enterprise systems into a single intelligent platform — turning factory data into actionable intelligence.

 06

Cycle Time Study & Optimization

Lean · Takt time · Bottleneck analysis

Cycle, lead and takt time analysis based on lean methodology — time-motion studies, bottleneck identification and process flow mapping that quantify inefficiencies across your line.

 07

Retro-fitting, Re-tooling & Relocation

Legacy modernisation · Line moves

Old or outdated equipment doesn't have to hold operations back. Diagnosis, modern upgrade proposals, SCADA integration, and complete relocation from disconnection to recalibration.

Global deployment · Training · AMC helpline

Machine and line commissioning, PLC/SCADA/robot debugging, field validation, safety testing, line balancing and production ramp-up — plus training and after-sales support that doesn't stop.
