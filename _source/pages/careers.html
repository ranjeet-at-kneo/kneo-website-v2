Why work here

Six reasons engineers
stay with us.

Automation is a craft. It rewards curiosity, patience and the willingness to be on a shop floor at 2am when a line has to run by morning.

Innovation & ideas

We encourage engineers to solve customer problems their own way. The best solution usually comes from whoever is closest to the machine.

Continuous learning

Education is treated as lifelong professional development, not a one-week onboarding course.

Global exposure

Projects span 30+ countries. Commissioning engineers travel, and see how manufacturing works in very different cultures.

Cutting-edge technology

Digital twins, IIoT platforms, AI-assisted vision and MES — alongside the PLC and robotics fundamentals that never go out of date.

Potential and experience both count

Growth opportunities at any career stage, with clear advancement pathways in technical and leadership tracks.

Industry leadership

A genuine hand in shaping how manufacturing digitalises — not a support role at the edge of it.
