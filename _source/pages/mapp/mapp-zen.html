Key capabilities

The complete quality suite.

Automated ballooning

PDF, PNG, JPG and TIFF drawings ballooned automatically, with characteristics extracted into a structured table.

Digital Process Flow Diagram

PFD linked directly with PFMEA and Control Plan, so the three documents cannot drift apart.

Mind-map PFMEA

Risk visualised as a structure rather than a spreadsheet row, making failure chains intuitive to review.

Connected control plans

Control plans synchronised with process risks and product characteristics automatically.

Digital inspection sheets

Operator-friendly interface with real-time quality checks at the point of inspection.

Machine vision integration

Integrated vision system support for automated visual inspection and defect detection in-cycle.

Online SPC

Live control charts, limits and trend monitoring rather than a retrospective analysis.

Defect-trend analytics

Compliance insights and defect pattern analysis across products, shifts and suppliers.

Revision management

Revision control, audit trail and version history across every linked quality document.

The quality thread

From drawing to PPAP, connected.

Benefits

What changes for the quality team.

  • Characteristics entered once and reused across every APQP document
  • Drawing revisions propagate instead of triggering a full re-key
  • Audit preparation becomes a report, not a project
  • Vision inspection results land in the same record as manual checks
  • SPC visible while the process is running, not after the batch
  • Defect trends analysed across products, shifts and suppliers
