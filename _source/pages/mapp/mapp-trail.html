The problem

A recall you cannot bound becomes a recall of everything.

When a defect is found in the field, the question is always the same: which other units are affected? Without genealogy the honest answer is "we don't know" — and the commercial response is to quarantine far more than necessary.

Traceability is insurance you buy before you need it. It also happens to be a customer requirement in most automotive, aerospace and FMCG supply agreements.

Key capabilities

What MAPP TRAIL does.

Product & process genealogy

End-to-end record of every operation, material and parameter applied to a serialised unit.

Real-time WIP tracking

Live position of work in progress across the line, including buffers and rework loops.

4M traceability

Man, machine, material and method recorded against every unit produced.

Backward & forward tracing

From a suspect batch to every affected finished unit, and back from a field failure to its inputs.

Process parameter recording

Torque, temperature, pressure, cycle time and any other critical parameter stored per unit.

Barcode, QR & RFID

Identification methods chosen to suit the part, the environment and the existing infrastructure.

Supplier-to-customer mapping

Material flow linked from goods receipt through to dispatch and customer delivery note.

Compliance-ready reports

Traceability reports formatted for customer and regulatory submission without manual assembly.

Benefits

What it is worth.

  • Containment bounded to the units genuinely affected
  • Field failures traced to root cause in hours rather than weeks
  • Customer traceability requirements met without manual record-keeping
  • Supplier quality issues attributed with evidence
  • Warranty claims defended with per-unit process data
  • Audit readiness maintained continuously rather than prepared for

Where it matters most: automotive and FMCG supply chains where a customer can demand full genealogy for a specific serial number at short notice.
