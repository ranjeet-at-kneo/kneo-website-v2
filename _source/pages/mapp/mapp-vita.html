The problem

Calendar maintenance is either too early or too late.

Fixed-interval servicing replaces components that had life left, and misses the ones that degraded faster than average. Both outcomes cost money — one in parts and downtime taken unnecessarily, the other in failures nobody saw coming.

The signals that distinguish the two are usually already available from the drive, the controller or an added sensor.

Reactive

Fix it after it breaks. Highest total cost.

Preventive

Fix it on a schedule. Predictable but wasteful.

Condition-based

Fix it when a measured parameter drifts.

Predictive

Fix it before the trend reaches the limit.

Key capabilities

What MAPP VITA does.

Preventive, breakdown & predictive

All three maintenance strategies managed in a single module with one asset register.

Condition monitoring

Continuous tracking of the parameters that actually indicate degradation on each asset.

Fault & downtime analysis

Failure history analysed by asset, mode and cause rather than logged and forgotten.

Tool & mould tracking

Life counting, change scheduling and location tracking for tooling and moulds.

Spare inventory tracking

Critical spares linked to the assets that consume them, with reorder visibility.

Digital work orders

Raised, assigned, executed and closed digitally with the history attached to the asset.

Utility monitoring

Compressed air, water, power and other utilities monitored for consumption and anomaly.

MTBF & MTTR analytics

Reliability and recovery metrics per asset, trended over time to show whether things are improving.

Benefits

What the maintenance team gains.

  • Unplanned downtime reduced by acting on degradation trends early
  • Maintenance labour directed to assets that need attention
  • Spare parts inventory sized against actual consumption
  • Tool changes scheduled against real usage rather than estimates
  • Reliability improvement demonstrated with MTBF and MTTR evidence
  • Utility anomalies surfaced before they become a cost or compliance issue

Better together: MAPP EYE supplies the machine performance history that makes predictive maintenance genuinely predictive rather than an educated guess.
