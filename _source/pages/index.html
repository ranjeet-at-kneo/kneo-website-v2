    Built to the OEM's spec, not retrofitted

    Tag naming, block structure, safety architecture and documentation follow the OEM's own specification from the first line of code — not retrofitted after a review.

    Why it matters

    How we work

    From first conversation
    to production ramp-up.

    A disciplined delivery model, refined across 400+ automation projects and validated by ISO 9001:2015 processes.

    STEP 01

    Discovery & feasibility

    We study the process, capture cycle-time and takt data, identify bottlenecks and agree measurable objectives before a single line of code is written.

    STEP 02

    Engineering & design

    Functional design specification, E-Plan schematics, panel layouts, safety architecture (SIL/PL) and I/O planning — all reviewed with your team.

    STEP 03

    Software development

    PLC logic, HMI/SCADA screens, robot programs, recipe management, alarms and historian — developed offline so your team stays focused on execution.

    STEP 04

    Virtual commissioning

    A digital twin catches collisions, sequence errors and logic faults in software, before the system ever reaches your shop floor.

    STEP 05

    Onsite commissioning

    Installation, I/O validation, safety testing, line balancing and production ramp-up support — anywhere in the world, with operator training included.

    STEP 06

    Support & continuous improvement

    Ongoing maintenance, troubleshooting, software updates and system enhancements, backed by annual maintenance contracts that include 24/7 remote support.
