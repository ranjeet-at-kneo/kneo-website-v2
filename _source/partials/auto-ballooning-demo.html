Step 1

Import the drawing

Load the drawing

Bring in your own drawing and balloon it. The file is read inside your browser — it is never uploaded and never leaves your computer.

This walkthrough uses a sample bracket drawing with two views, notes and a title block.

Works best on a PDF exported from CAD, where the dimension text is real text. A scanned sheet has no text to read, so use Manual map on it.
