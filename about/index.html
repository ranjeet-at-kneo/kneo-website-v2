Why we exist

Automation that makes manufacturing sustainable, not just faster.

KNEO Automation has operated since 2003, delivering industrial automation solutions for over two decades. What began as a controls engineering practice now spans robotics, MES, IIoT and full digital transformation programmes for manufacturers on four continents.

Our Vision

To create a world where intelligent automation and digitalization enable sustainable, efficient, and seamlessly connected manufacturing ecosystems.

Our Mission

Deploying smart automation and digital solutions that enhance efficiency, reduce waste, and accelerate the transition toward sustainable, future-ready operations.

What we believe

The KNEO Manifesto

01

Everything under one roof

Automation and Industry 4.0 expertise in a single partner — from electrical design to PLC code to cloud dashboards. No handoffs lost between vendors.

02

Visibility before everything

After two decades inside manufacturing facilities, one pattern repeats: visibility is always the first problem to solve. We instrument before we optimise.

03

Prove it virtually, then build it

Collisions, sequence faults and logic errors belong in a digital twin — not on your shop floor during a production window.

04

Brand-agnostic by principle

Siemens, Rockwell, Beckhoff, Mitsubishi, Delta, KUKA, FANUC, ABB, Yaskawa. We speak the language of your installed base rather than selling you a new one.

05

The project does not end at handover

Ongoing support, maintenance, troubleshooting, software updates and system enhancements. When you need us, our team is responsive, accessible and ready to help.

Standards & compliance

Engineered to recognised norms.

Our internal PLC & HMI programming guidelines are aligned to international norms, so the code we hand over is maintainable by your team — or anyone else's.

  • ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management
  • IEC 61131-3 programming languages
  • IEC 81346-1 designation structures
  • CE, UL, IEC & OSHA design compliance
  • Safety circuit design to SIL / PL levels
  • AIAG-VDA aligned quality workflows
  • AS9102 / VDA reporting formats
